## ⚙️ Usage

Once the app opens, you’ll see a clean window with three main sections:

### 1️⃣ Select Your Photoshop
- The app auto-detects your Photoshop installation. If not, click **"Browse"** and locate `Photoshop.exe` (usually in `C:\Program Files\Adobe\Adobe Photoshop [version]\`).

### 2️⃣ Choose a Task
- **Thumbnail Resizer** — resizes any image to YouTube’s ideal 1280×720 px.
- **Text Overlay** — adds your channel name or title text with a preset font and shadow.
- **Brand Colors** — applies your saved color palette to the entire image (one-click adjustment layers).

### 3️⃣ Pick Your Files
- Click **"Add Images"** to select one or many images (Ctrl+click for multiple).
- For each image, you’ll see a preview. Toggle options on/off (e.g., "add border", "blur background").

### ▶️ Run
- Hit **"Start Processing"**. The app will open Photoshop in the background (you’ll see it flash), apply the changes, and save new files into a subfolder named `_yt_processed` inside your original image folder.

**That’s it.** Your new thumbnails are ready to upload.

> 💡 Tip: You can leave the app open while editing other things — it works in the background.

## 🛠️ Troubleshooting

### "App won’t start"
- Make sure you **extracted** the .zip — don’t double-click the .zip itself.
- Right-click the `.exe` → **"Run as administrator"** (if you get a permission error).

### "Photoshop not found"
- Reopen the app and click **"Browse"** → navigate to your Photoshop install folder. Make sure you pick the actual `Photoshop.exe` file (not a shortcut).

### "No images processed"
- Check that your images are **JPEG, PNG, or BMP** format.
- Ensure the destination folder is writable (not on a read‑only drive like a CD).

### "Photoshop opens but nothing happens"
- Close Photoshop completely, then restart the tool. Some older versions require a clean state.

### "Slow processing"
- For large batches, close other heavy apps (browsers, games). The tool uses Photoshop’s scripting engine, which is CPU-intensive.

### "Windows SmartScreen warning"
- Click **"More info"** → **"Run anyway"**. This is normal for unsigned open-source tools.
